Upload Parking photographs

Here you will upload photographs of the on-street parking you intend to use for your parking.

Staff will use these to assess your parking evidence

Upload your photographs below ensuring they meet the requirements listed below.

Before you take your photographs

  • Make sure your parking complies with the road restrictions and signs that apply to the street.
  • Ensure the parking is shown in relation to your property.
  • Photographs must be clear and in good lightning.
  • Do put yourself or others at risk when obtaining your photographs.

What an example set should show

A wider view should show your property and the proposed parking area together. A closer view should ensure that any parking signs or roads markings are readable.

If one photograph cannot show the full context, include another view. A photograph alone does not confirm that the parking is compliant or that your application will be approved.

Ensure photographs are clear and are without distortion or blur which could impact our ability to review these images.
